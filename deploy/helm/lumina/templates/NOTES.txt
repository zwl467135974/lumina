Lumina AI Agent Platform 已部署！

1. 等待所有 Pod 就绪:
   kubectl get pods -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}

2. 获取前端访问地址:
{{- if .Values.ingress.enabled }}
   export LUMINA_URL=http://{{ .Values.ingress.host }}
{{- else }}
   kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "lumina.fullname" . }}-frontend 8080:80
   export LUMINA_URL=http://localhost:8080
{{- end }}

3. 访问 $LUMINA_URL 开始使用 Lumina。

部署的服务:
  - Gateway:        svc/{{ include "lumina.fullname" . }}-gateway:8080
  - Business Base:  svc/{{ include "lumina.fullname" . }}-business-base:8082
  - Agent Service:  svc/{{ include "lumina.fullname" . }}-agent-service:8081
  - Frontend:       svc/{{ include "lumina.fullname" . }}-frontend:80
